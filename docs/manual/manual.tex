\chapter{Overview}\label{ch:overview}
akeyless-watchover manages the governance of an Akeyless account as code: paths, access roles,
auth methods and sub-claims, account settings, PKI, event forwarders and Gateway administration,
modelled in YAML and delivered by Terraform from one repository. It has two interfaces:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
& \textbf{Skill commands} (Chapter~\ref{ch:skill}) & \textbf{Kit commands} (Chapter~\ref{ch:kit}) \\ \midrule
Used by & an AI agent (any agent and model) & you, CI, or an agent with a shell / MCP \\
Syntax & \texttt{akeyless-watchover <command> [request]} & \texttt{scripts/watchover <command> [options]} \\
Produces & designs, model diffs, reviews, exact commands & tests, simulations, exports, scaffolds, packages \\
Runs where & in the conversation & in the governance repository \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Notation} \texttt{<x>} required, \texttt{[x]} optional, \texttt{a|b} choice; \emph{repeatable}
options may be given several times. \emph{Live} commands call Akeyless; all others are offline.
\textbf{VERIFY} marks a fact no primary source confirms. Exit codes: 0 success, 1 check failed,
2 usage or execution error (per command: each entry).

\chapter{Set-up}\label{ch:setup}
\section{Kit}
Requirements: Python 3.10+ with \texttt{pyyaml} (and \texttt{pytest} for \texttt{test}); Terraform
$\geq$ 1.7 or OpenTofu $\geq$ 1.8; for live commands the \texttt{akeyless} CLI or \texttt{curl}+\texttt{jq}
(\texttt{token}). Copy \texttt{terraform/}, \texttt{bootstrap/}, \texttt{scripts/} and the CI template
from \texttt{ci/} into your governance repository; check with \texttt{scripts/watchover test}.
